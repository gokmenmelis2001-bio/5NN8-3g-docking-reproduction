# Figures

Figures in this directory were independently generated from the molecular docking reproduction workflow.

## Figure descriptions

### Figure 1 — Blind docking result
`figure1_3g_blind_docking_result.png`

AutoDock Vina blind docking results for compound 3g against rhGAA (PDB 5NN8).  
The best-scoring pose obtained in the blind docking stage had a predicted affinity of approximately **−8.87 kcal/mol**.

### Figure 2 — Focused docking result
`figure2_3g_focused_docking_result.png`

Focused docking results for compound 3g within the catalytic region of rhGAA.  
The best-scoring pose had a predicted affinity of approximately **−9.06 kcal/mol**.

### Figure 3 — 3D binding interactions
`figure3_3g_3d_binding_interactions.png`

3D visualization of compound 3g and surrounding interacting residues within the rhGAA binding pocket.

### Figure 4 — Binding pose in rhGAA
`figure4_3g_binding_pose_in_rhGAA.png`

Overall 3D view of the reproduced docking pose of compound 3g within the rhGAA structure.
